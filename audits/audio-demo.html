Dust Cloaks - audio listening demo

Standalone page: no game code. Everything is made live with Web Audio. Use headphones or decent speakers; the problem frequencies are around 800-2000 Hz. Click Start first (browsers need a click before audio).

1. Timbre: current pipe vs proposed lute (same notes)

Same phrase (Embers, first two bars, A minor). "Pipe" is the game's current melody voice re-created from the code (sine + octave triangle, 5 Hz vibrato, breath chiff, no reverb). "Lute" is a Karplus-Strong string, filtered, with a touch of room.

2. The theme: Town → Contract, one tune that builds

One looping 8-bar melody. Hit a preset, or switch layers by hand while it plays. The tempo changes smoothly over about two bars, like Humanity's time speed-up.

5. J-20 as built: one continuous theme (camp → contract → camp)

What the game does now: the tune the bard is playing never stops when a contract starts. Layers join or leave only on bar lines (level 0 nobody near, 1 contact, 2 boss / crowd / Leader hurt) and the tempo eases toward a target (Game speed, slow-mo, fight level, Momentum rank; range 0.7×-1.35×). Camp stays slow. "Run" plays a simulated outing: camp, contract starts, contact, boss, calm, back to camp (about 25 bars). Or drag the Danger slider yourself.

4. J-19 as built (what is now in the game, js/audio.js)

Same two bars as section 1. "As built" uses the exact in-game values: lute peak 0.24 (lowpass 3600→900 Hz), frame drum dum/tek with small random variation, room reverb wet 0.14, high shelf −2 dB. "Old town" is the pipe plus the old thump/tick, dry. The last button adds the wind bed and fire crackle that now fill the rests between verses.

6. J-21 as built: one context, ducking, sliders

In the game, sound effects and music now share one audio context, so important effects can dip the music (−3 to −6 dB, 30 ms down, about half a second back). Start the theme (section 2, Play), then try the buttons. Horn, win, level-up, boss and Leader hurt duck the music; coin, pick and hits never do. Effects and Music are separate sliders in Settings, next to Volume.

7. J-22 as built: contexts and new feedback sounds

Title screen: the camp theme as a solo lute, 15 % slower and quieter, no drums. Debrief: a short lute stinger instead of the fanfare, then the camp theme carries on. New quiet sounds: a wood knock when a building starts, a crumble when one is demolished, soft tocks for pause and resume, a faint heartbeat while the Leader is at a third of Con or less, and a small ping when Momentum gives +1 Luck.
