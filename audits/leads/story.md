# Leads for the story designer (from an external Gemini playtest of Index 80)

Gemini's report has no story findings as such, but its onboarding proposal is largely a narrative restructure of the prologue. Evaluate it from the story side and fold your view into your report.

## The problem it describes
"The Founding of the Dust Cloaks" prologue makes the player choose campaign length, build a Squad Leader, pick four companions and shop an 850gp charter kit before they ever move or fight, then drops them on the Varnholt map with every system visible at once.

## The narrative beats it proposes
1. **Cold open, in media res.** The player picks one of three ready-made archetypes (Vanguard Soldier, Folk Archer, Battle Cleric) and is dropped straight into a 45-second ambush on the road outside Varnholt's gates. Character building comes later (or via an "advanced custom build" for veterans).
2. **Entering Varnholt.** The Reeve welcomes the squad inside and asks for one specific job (scout the north road, or rally the militia) — the first Work Order and the first visible dice roll.
3. **First failed roll → Luck.** A story moment that teaches spending vs burning Luck.
4. **First real wound in Assault 1.** A moment that teaches Wounds as lasting injuries.
5. **First night in camp.** Condition, supplies and the market arrive with the camp.

Note: Gemini calls the Reeve "Hallinan"; in the game he is "Reeve Aldous Harl" (`newStory`). Check names against the game and the design documents.

## What we need from you
- Does a cold open fit the prologue's existing story ("Year 616… the Frost-Vault legions… Varnholt… the Grey Cloaks")? Write a short draft of the opening ambush framing (3–6 lines) in the game's plain, dry voice, and of the Reeve's first request.
- Which beats can reuse existing text and events, and which need new writing?
- Risks: anything in the current prologue (structures, districts, days, assaults, the road part) that the restructure would break.
- Recommend the smallest version worth doing first.
