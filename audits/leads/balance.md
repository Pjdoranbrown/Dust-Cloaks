# Leads for the game balancer (from an external Gemini playtest of Index 80)

Gemini's report has no balance findings, so your original brief stands. Two related questions from its onboarding proposal are worth your view:

1. **Archetype starts.** Gemini proposes three ready-made starting Squad Leaders for new players (Vanguard Soldier, Folk Archer, Battle Cleric). If we did this, what race/class/specialty/gear combinations would be fair, easy to play and representative? Suggest exact builds that are neither the strongest nor traps.
2. **The 850gp charter kit in the prologue.** Is 850gp across melee weapons, armour and specialist kits a sensible first shopping decision, or should the prologue start with a fixed sensible kit (with the market opened later)? What would that default kit be?

Also note for context: the co-op multiplayer design in the report (wave budget × (1 + 0.65 × (players − 1)), smaller retinues per player) is NOT being pursued yet — no need to review it.
